Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Cancellation

  • You can cancel an order any time before payment by pressing "Cancel" in the bot.
  • If you cancel after paying but before we start work, we refund the full amount.

Refunds

  • If we are unable to deliver your order, you get a full refund.
  • If the delivered file does not match what you ordered (wrong class, subject or topic), we will correct and resend it; if we cannot, you get a full refund.
  • Because our products are digital and made to order, we do not refund once the correct file has been delivered.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days.
